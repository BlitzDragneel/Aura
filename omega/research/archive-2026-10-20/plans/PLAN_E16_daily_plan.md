# PLAN E16 — forward record of OMEGA's Daily Plan (v6.5.91), judged once
Written 2026-10-22 before the Daily Plan exists. What it is: for BTC, ETH, SOL, BNB and XRP, a side chosen from the market
state (E8's definitions on the five coins' own daily index) and the coin's 200-hour average, and an entry at the nearest
liquidity level on the with-trend side (Market Read's pools), stop 0.5 hourly range beyond that level, target the nearest
pool on the other side; shown only when reward-to-risk ≥ 1.5 and the entry is within 3 hourly ranges. Grade C (unproven:
the trend and sweep rules it resembles failed fair tests). A plan becomes a paper trade when an hourly bar touches the entry;
it closes at the stop, the target, or after 48 hours; costs 0.12% round trip; results in R.
Judged once, after 50 closed paper trades or 26 weeks, whichever comes first: works if the average R is positive with t ≥ 1.65
(trades clustered by week). Until then it is labelled unproven. Reported whatever happens.
