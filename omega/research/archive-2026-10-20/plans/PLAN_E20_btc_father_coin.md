# PLAN E20 — Bitcoin as the father coin: do BTC's warnings come before the altcoins fall? (written before running)
Written 2026-10-25. Data: BTC daily closes (omega_history_creed15_1) and the 20 majors (creed15_2–5: ADA, DOGE, AVAX, LINK,
LTC, TRX, DOT, BCH, NEAR, ATOM, UNI, APT, ARB, OP, SUI, FIL, ICP, ETC, XLM, INJ), Binance spot, daily (UTC), 2020 – Oct 2026.
Alt basket: equal weight of the coins with data each day (daily rebalanced).
Warnings, computed from BTC alone at each day's close and acted on from the next day:
  W1 (primary): BTC below its 200-day average.   W2: BTC 15%+ below its 90-day high.   W3: BTC's 50-day average below its 200-day.
Tests:
 (1) Next 30 days of the alt basket when W1 is on versus off, in non-overlapping 30-day blocks: off minus on must be positive
     with t ≥ 2.0 (whole period).
 (2) The gate: hold the basket only while W1 is off (cash otherwise; 0.10% per switch) versus holding it throughout. It must
     have a higher return per unit of risk (Sharpe) AND a smaller maximum drawdown, in BOTH 2020-07 → 2023-12 and 2024 → 2026-10.
     (The first 200 days are needed for the average.)
 PASS = (1) and (2) for W1. W2 and W3 reported, not tested.
 (3) Reported: does BTC lead? Correlation of BTC's daily return with the basket's return 0–5 days later, and the reverse.
Reported whatever happens.
