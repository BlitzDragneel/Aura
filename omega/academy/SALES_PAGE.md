# OMEGA Academy — Trade with evidence

**A trading course that shows you what was tested, what failed, and how to test for yourself before you risk money.**

Most trading courses sell you a system. This one starts by showing you what happened when thousands of systems were tested fairly. OMEGA's research checked 12,480 daily trading rules, about 11,000 setups from a popular-style top-down method, and more than 1,300 further variants in November 2026. Every plan was written down before the data was looked at, every attempt was counted, costs were always included, and each final test was run only once.

Almost everything failed. Two setups passed, and both are fading.

That is the most useful thing a new trader can learn, and this course is built around it.
