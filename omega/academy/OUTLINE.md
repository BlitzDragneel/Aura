# OMEGA Academy — Trade with evidence — course outline

Version 1.0, updated 2026-11-08. 10 modules, 57 lessons, about 399 minutes of reading (≈6.7 hours) plus exercises in the OMEGA app. Every lesson has key points and a 4-question quiz with explanations.

**Free:** Modules 1–2 in full, plus the first lesson of every other module (19 lessons, about 137 minutes). **Premium:** the other 38 lessons (about 262 minutes).

Minutes are estimated from lesson length (about 140 words a minute, plus time for the worked examples).
